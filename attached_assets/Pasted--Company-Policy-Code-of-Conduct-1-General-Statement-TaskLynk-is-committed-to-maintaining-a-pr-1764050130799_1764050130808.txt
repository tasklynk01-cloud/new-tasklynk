📘 Company Policy & Code of Conduct
1. General Statement
TaskLynk is committed to maintaining a professional, transparent, and ethical academic-support and freelance work environment. All users—Clients, Freelancers, Managers, Editors, and Admins—must comply with the policies outlined below. Violation of these policies may lead to account restrictions, penalties, or permanent ban.
________________________________________
2. Client Policies
2.1 Fraud & Misrepresentation
Clients must avoid:
•	Filing fraudulent disputes
•	Submitting false claims about work quality
•	Refusing to pay for completed and approved work
•	Uploading fake evidence to force refunds
Penalty:
Immediate account suspension, permanent ban, and legal follow-up for financial fraud.
________________________________________
2.2 Payment & Refund Policy
•	Payment must be completed before order activation or release.
•	Partial refunds are only issued if the platform confirms service failure.
•	Full refunds are not issued when:
o	Work has been delivered according to instructions
o	The client becomes unresponsive
o	The client changes requirements after completion
Chargebacks:
Chargebacks will result in account termination and debt collection.
________________________________________
2.3 Duplicate or Fraudulent Accounts
Clients may not create multiple accounts to:
•	Circumvent payment limits
•	Abuse discounts
•	Hide previous bans
Penalty:
All duplicate accounts are merged and suspended; ongoing orders are placed on hold; potential legal action for fraud.
________________________________________
2.4 Abuse, Harassment & Threats
•	Threatening managers, freelancers, or support staff is strictly prohibited.
•	Abusive or manipulative behavior to force free work is not allowed.
Penalty:
Temporary or permanent suspension.
________________________________________
________________________________________
3. Freelancer Policies
3.1 Academic Integrity
Freelancers must:
•	Deliver original work
•	Cite sources properly
•	Produce plagiarism-free content
Prohibited:
•	Copy-pasting from the internet
•	Submitting work generated by AI without disclosure (when AI is not permitted)
•	Reusing content from past orders
Penalty:
•	1st offense: 30% fine deducted + written warning
•	2nd offense: 100% fine + 14-day suspension
•	3rd offense: Permanent ban
________________________________________
3.2 AI Content Policy
Use of AI tools (ChatGPT, Bard, Claude, etc.) is:
•	Allowed only when permitted by the client/admin
•	Banned for technical, professional, or strict academic assignments unless specified
All final deliverables MUST include:
•	AI-detection report
•	Plagiarism report
Submitting AI-generated content without approval is misconduct.
Penalty: Treated as plagiarism.
________________________________________
3.3 Deadline & Availability
Freelancers must:
•	Deliver drafts, revisions, and final copies on time
•	Inform managers early if a delay is expected
•	Be reachable during the order lifecycle
Penalty for late delivery:
•	10–50% deduction depending on severity
•	Order reassigned and freelancer unpaid for repeated lateness
•	Suspension after multiple late submissions
________________________________________
3.4 Fraud & Account Abuse
Freelancers may NOT:
•	Use multiple accounts
•	Sell or rent accounts
•	Collude with clients for external payments
•	Manipulate ratings or feedback
Penalty: Immediate and permanent ban + forfeiture of earnings.
________________________________________
3.5 Corruption & Abuse of Power
Freelancers may NOT:
•	Bribe managers for priority assignments
•	Offer gifts or money for unfair treatment
•	Manipulate queue systems
Penalty: Permanent termination.
________________________________________
________________________________________
4. Manager / Editor Policies
4.1 Abuse of Power
Managers are prohibited from:
•	Favoring specific freelancers unfairly
•	Assigning orders to unqualified persons
•	Taking bribes or engaging in corruption
•	Editing client instructions to manipulate outcomes
Penalty:
•	1st offense: Salary deduction + written warning
•	2nd offense: Suspension
•	3rd offense: Permanent removal from the team
________________________________________
4.2 Quality Control Responsibilities
Managers must:
•	Verify plagiarism and AI reports
•	Approve deliveries only after confirming quality
•	Ensure instructions are clear before assigning
•	Request additional pay only when justified
Penalty for negligence:
Fines or removal depending on severity.
________________________________________
4.3 Confidentiality Policy
Managers must protect:
•	Client personal data
•	Attached documents
•	Research materials
Any leak of client documents is grounds for immediate dismissal.
________________________________________
________________________________________
5. Admin & Internal Staff Policy
5.1 Integrity & Neutrality
Admins must:
•	Handle disputes impartially
•	Avoid favoritism
•	Protect all user data
Any intentional mismanagement is treated as corruption.
________________________________________
5.2 System Access & Security
Admins must not:
•	Grant unauthorized users access
•	Modify database records without documentation
•	Delete financial logs without approval
Violations may result in criminal liability.
________________________________________
________________________________________
6. Payment & Earnings Policy
6.1 Freelancer Payments
•	Payments are released after client approval or auto-approval window.
•	Earnings are calculated after platform fees and penalties.
•	Withdrawals follow the set schedule (Mpesa, bank transfer, etc.).
6.2 Manager Payments
Managers are paid:
•	Fixed acceptance fee
•	Submission fee
•	Extra-page fee
Late reviews or low oversight may reduce payouts.
________________________________________
6.3 Refund Impact
If an order is refunded:
•	Freelancer payment may be reduced or canceled depending on fault
•	Managers may lose management fees
•	Sensitive cases handled manually by admin
________________________________________
________________________________________
7. Prohibited Activities
The following will result in immediate account deletion:
7.1 Selling or Transferring Accounts
Strictly prohibited.
7.2 External Payments
No user may request payment outside the platform:
•	Mpesa
•	WhatsApp
•	Telegram
•	Bank transfers
7.3 Cheating the System
•	Fake documents
•	Fake reports
•	Manipulating payment pages
•	Creating multiple accounts for earnings or discount abuse
________________________________________
________________________________________
8. Confidentiality & Data Protection
All parties must:
•	Keep order details private
•	Maintain academic confidentiality
•	Avoid storing client documents on unsecured devices
Data privacy violations may lead to legal actions.
________________________________________
________________________________________

________________________________________
10. Final Clause
By using the platform, every user agrees to be bound by these policies. TaskLynk reserves the right to update policies at any time to maintain platform integrity, improve service quality, and ensure a secure environment for all users.
